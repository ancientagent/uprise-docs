Here is the full breakdown for what we offer artists as far as paid promotional services that make up part of our business model.  
There are two different promotional avenues an artist can take to get the word out about something new they're involved in. These two avenues are "Street Team" and "Over The Air"

1. "Street Team" is your traditional flyer style method of promotion that is used in most social media platforms whereas, the announcement promoted will appear periodically throughout the feeds of whatever communities the promotion was posted to for as long as the promotion was paid to run.  
There are two types of "Street Team" promotion packages a user can purchase.
      a. "Local Promotions" : Artists will purchase this promotional package when they want to promote either a new single they've released or an event that they are playing. Initially these would run @$5 a day or $20 a week, however artists with paid subscriptions will either receive discounted promotion costs or a set amount included per month. All local promotions post to the community feed of the artists' citywide home scene (can choose to post in statewide for $x2)   For singles, the artist would select the single they are looking to promote, how long they would like it to run it and check a box if they want it to run statewide for $x2   When users click on it they will be taken to the artist's page where the song will begin playing giving the user the opportunity to "add" song to their personal collection. For events, artists will select the event they would like to promote, how long, etc.. when users click on it they will be taken to the event description in the event page where users can choose to "add" to their calendar. 

(Users who are already following the artist will receive a personal notification upon release of single and/or creation of event, in both cases they will be added automatically to their collection and / or calendar). 
**Note this has nothing to do with "promotional packages"  Its just another promotional aspect that is part of the "Follow" feature
      
     b.  "Tour Promotions" Artists can purchase a Tour Promo that gives communities the chance to get to know the artist a bit before they come to their town. This will include a custom event flyer that will be added to the communities' event sections as well as a feature in the "on tour" carousel in each feed of the communities they tour through up to a month in advance, in which user can click to visit their profile and here their latest singles.  
**Note all events posted in a community's event section will include the artists who are playing the event, if the artist is on the platform users viewing the event will be able to visit the artists profile by clicking on their name.   

2. "Over the Air" promotions: a personalized 7-10 second audio based promotions artists can record and upload to their rotation stack promoting whatever they would like. They can either record or upload the file and attach it to a song for listeners of the community fair-play RaDIYo to hear. businesses will be able to take advantage of this by paying artists for their on-air airspace by giving them a code or file to upload. This would be channeled in through  the communities' promotional page as part of the business account suite.
