HOW IT WORKS
UPRISE is more than a music platform, it's a movement to reclaim music independence from corporate dominance, built upon two cornerstones:


Uprise:

A seed of musical revolution that has taken root within the UPRISE ecosystem
A digital community defined by genre and location where artists and fans authentically engage with one another

Uprises exist across 3 interconnected tiers:

Citywide: The foundation where all music originates (e.g., "Los Angeles Punk Uprise")
Statewide: The collective voice of all citywide communities (e.g., "Texas Folk Uprise")
Nationwide: The unified presence of all statewide support (e.g., "U.S. Hip-Hop Uprise")

Home Scene:
While scenes typically refer to the location (city/state) in which an uprise exists, Home scenes can refer to both the registered city/state of residence, and the genre of choice, or rather the marriage of the two as 1 specialized uprise.  Home scenes are where listeners have voting power and is where artists release their music, and serves as the default uprise in which users operate.

An uprise represents the reclamation of musical discovery from corporate gatekeepers, returning power to the people who create and truly love the music.


RaDIYo:

The circulation system of the movement, channeling music from the grassroots of neighborhoods to the broader cultural landscape
A revolutionary "do it yourself" distribution engine that gives artists equal opportunity to be heard

RaDIYo broadcasts across 3 interconnected tiers:

Citywide: Contains all music submitted by local artists to their community. This is where every song begins its journey.

Statewide: Contains only the most upvoted songs that have risen up from all citywide uprises within that state. Songs here have proven their appeal to local audiences.

Nationwide: Contains only the most upvoted songs that have risen up from all statewide rotations. These represent the true cream of the crop, selected by listeners across the country.

Voting: Users can upvote songs during play, collectively determining which music advances to broader audiences. This democratic process ensures that popularity stems from genuine community response.

Fair Play is the algorithmic backbone ensuring all songs begin with equal opportunity, rising or falling based solely on listener engagement—not money, fame, or connections.

Together, uprises and raDIYo create a self-sustaining ecosystem where music flows naturally from local to national audiences based on merit, rebuilding the connection between artists and listeners that has been severed by corporate intermediaries.
