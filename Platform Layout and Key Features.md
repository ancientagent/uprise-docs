# Uprise Platform Layout and Key Features

## Main Sections
1. Home
2. Discover

## Home Section
Divided into 5 tabs:

### 1. Feed (Default)
- Live updates of community activities
- Recommended content based on user preferences and taste tags
- "Blast" feature for users to share enjoyed tracks
- Notifications for followed artists' new events and music

### 2. Events
- Upcoming on-platform events created by artists
- Automatic calendar sync for followed artists' events

### 3. Promotions
- Ad space for local businesses and music promoters
- Targeted ads for local businesses and mainstream artists

### 4. Statistics
- Community-wide statistics
- "Activity Score" feature tracking user engagement

### 5. Social (Planned for future release)
- Message boards for community engagement
- Only place for direct user-to-user communication

## Discover Section
- Continues playing current station if in citywide tier
- Option to type in a new city or state to explore
- Upon selecting a location:
  * Player starts music from that citywide or statewide community
  * Link provided to visit the selected community
  * Recommendation categories update to reflect the location
- Swiping feature:
  * Left to right: Loads random statewide broadcast of user's favorite genre
  * Right to left: Loads random broadcast from a neighboring city
- Users can favorite discovered citywide and statewide broadcasts
- Content carousels with categories including:
  * Citywide Communities
  * Statewide Communities
  * Popular Songs from around the country
  * Popular Artists from around the country
- "Vibe Score" system (planned feature) for personalized recommendations
- Access to discovery beyond home communities will require paid membership (free during beta)

## Community Structure

### Home Scene
- Users are automatically placed in a community based on their registered genre and home city
- This becomes the user's "Home Scene"

### Broadcast Network
- Each home scene has a 3-tiered broadcast network:
  * Citywide
  * Statewide
  * Nationwide
- Users (both community members and visitors) can tune in to any of these tiers

### Citywide Tier
- All songs played on the citywide tier are uploaded by artists within that community
- Fair Play system governs song rotation, ensuring no artist has an unfair advantage
- Provides every independent artist an opportunity to be discovered, starting with their own community

## Future Features
### Mixologist
- Paid account upgrade for listeners
- Creation of limited playlists/compilations from discovered artists
- Ability to sell playlists on "Mix Market"
- Revenue split: 20% to Mixologist, rest split between featured artists

## User Registration
- Required information:
  * City and state of residence
  * Most connected genre
  * Artist registration status

## Additional Features
- Taste questionnaire in user profile for better recommendations
- Integration with other music platforms (Spotify, SoundCloud) for taste analysis
